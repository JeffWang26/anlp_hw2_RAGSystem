Carnegie Mellon School of Drama
is the first degree-granting
drama institution
in the United States of America.
Founded in 1914 and located in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, it is one of five schools within the
Carnegie Mellon College of Fine Arts
The school's undergraduate
programs in acting, musical theatre, directing, design, dramaturgy, and
production technology and management majors are considered to be among
the top programs in
conservatory training. Its
offerings in directing, design, dramatic writing, and production and technology management are also considered to be top
graduate programs
The School of Drama offers 18 events every season on campus, and also
presents members of its graduating class in produced showcases in
. Many Carnegie Mellon graduates have also gone on to successful careers in
Pittsburgh theatre
