Bridges: Pittsburgh is known as the "City of Bridges" because it has 446 bridges, more than any other city in the world, including Venice.
City of Champions (1979): The year 1979 is incredibly significant for Pittsburgh sports. The city earned the nickname "City of Champions" because the Pittsburgh Steelers won Super Bowl XIII and the Pittsburgh Pirates won the World Series in the exact same year.
Roberto Clemente Bridge: The Roberto Clemente Bridge is a historic suspension bridge named after the Pittsburgh Pirates baseball legend Roberto Clemente, who tragically died in a plane crash while delivering aid to earthquake victims.
