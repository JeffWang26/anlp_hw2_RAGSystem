Gaucho Parrilla Argentina: Located in downtown Pittsburgh, Gaucho Parrilla Argentina is a highly popular restaurant that serves authentic Argentine wood-fired meats and steaks.

Pusadee's Garden: Pusadee's Garden is an upscale restaurant located in the Lawrenceville neighborhood of Pittsburgh, specializing in elevated Thai cuisine served in a beautiful, expansive courtyard setting.

Morcilla: Located in the Lawrenceville neighborhood, Morcilla is a highly acclaimed restaurant that serves traditional Spanish pintxos, tapas, and charcuterie.

DiAnoia's Eatery: DiAnoia's Eatery in the Strip District is an all-day Italian cafe that specializes in homemade pastas, Italian pastries, and serves as a wine bar at night.

The Eagle: The Eagle is a food and beer hall located in downtown Pittsburgh that specializes in Southern cuisine, particularly known for its fried chicken and southern sides.

Apteka: Apteka is a popular vegan restaurant located on Penn Avenue in the Bloomfield/Garfield area, serving 100% plant-based Central and Eastern European cuisine, including vegan pierogies.

Noodlehead: Noodlehead is a popular, cash-only restaurant in the Shadyside neighborhood that specializes in Thai street noodle dishes.

täkō: täkō is a vibrant restaurant in the downtown Cultural District of Pittsburgh that serves Asian-Mexican fusion cuisine, specifically focusing on inventive gourmet tacos.

Dish Osteria Bar: Dish Osteria Bar is an intimate dining spot located in the South Side neighborhood that serves traditional Sicilian and Italian cuisine.

Fet Fisk: Fet Fisk is a renowned Pittsburgh restaurant that evolved from a popular pop-up, specializing in Nordic-inspired seafood and seasonal dishes.

The Vandal: Located in Lawrenceville, The Vandal is a modern cafe and restaurant known for its Eastern European-inspired small plates and craft cocktails.

Driftwood Oven: Driftwood Oven is a beloved bakery and pizzeria in Lawrenceville that specializes in sourdough pizza and artisanal baked goods.

Tessaro's: Tessaro's is a historic restaurant in Bloomfield that is famous for its hardwood-grilled burgers and classic American pub food.

Bitter Ends Garden & Luncheonette: Bitter Ends Garden & Luncheonette was a highly regarded farm-to-table cafe in Bloomfield known for its locally sourced ingredients and fresh pastries.

Pittsburgh Salad: The traditional "Pittsburgh Salad" is uniquely known for being topped with french fries and grilled meat (usually steak or chicken). Restaurants like The Yard are famous for serving variations of it.

Pamela's Diner: Pamela's Diner is a classic Pittsburgh breakfast institution, most famous for its crepe-style pancakes and its "chipped chopped ham" breakfast specials.

Iconic Desserts: Prantl's Bakery in Pittsburgh is nationally famous for inventing the Burnt Almond Torte. For macarons, Gaby et Jules in Squirrel Hill is renowned for its authentic French macarons.
