Pittsburgh Taco Festival: The Pittsburgh Taco Festival is an annual food festival celebrating tacos, Latin-American culture, and the local Hispanic community, featuring dozens of food trucks and live entertainment.
Great American Banana Split Celebration: The Great American Banana Split Celebration is an annual festival held in Latrobe, near Pittsburgh, celebrating the invention of the banana split dessert in that town in 1904.
Lenten Fish Fry: The Lenten Fish Fry is a massive cultural tradition in Western Pennsylvania. Every Friday during the season of Lent, local churches, fire halls, and restaurants serve massive fried fish sandwiches to the community.
Pittsburgh VegFest: Pittsburgh VegFest is an annual summer festival, usually held in the Northside or Oakland, showcasing local vegan and vegetarian restaurants, food vendors, and animal rescue organizations.
Shopping Corridors: Walnut Street is the major upscale shopping and dining corridor located in the Shadyside neighborhood. The Waterworks Shopping Plaza is a large, open-air commercial shopping center located near Aspinwall. Penn Avenue is a major street that has transformed into a vibrant center for arts, dining, and music, particularly in the Garfield and Cultural District areas.
The Strip District: The Strip District is Pittsburgh's historic market district, famous for its wholesale grocers, international food markets, street vendors, and culinary diversity.
Wholey's: Wholey's Fish Market is an iconic, historic seafood market located in the Strip District, famous for selling fresh fish, seafood, and large fish sandwiches.
Hellbender Vinyl: Hellbender Vinyl is a prominent vinyl record pressing plant located in Pittsburgh, known for manufacturing records for independent artists and labels.
